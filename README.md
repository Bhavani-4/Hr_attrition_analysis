# 🌟 Project Overview

Organizations lose valuable talent every year, but understanding **why employees leave** is the first step toward improving retention.

This project transforms raw HR data into a professional **Business Intelligence Dashboard** that enables HR teams and decision-makers to identify workforce trends, monitor attrition, and make data-driven decisions.

The project covers the complete Data Analytics lifecycle—from cleaning raw data to delivering interactive dashboards with meaningful business insights.

---

# 🎯 Business Questions Solved

✔ Which department experiences the highest attrition?

✔ Does employee salary influence resignation?

✔ Which age groups leave the organization most frequently?

✔ How does work experience impact attrition?

✔ Which job roles require HR attention?

✔ What workforce patterns help improve employee retention?

# 📈 Key Insights

### 🏢 Department Analysis

Research & Development department recorded the highest employee attrition.

---

### 💰 Salary Analysis

Employees earning lower monthly salaries are more likely to leave the company.

---

### ⏳ Experience Analysis

Most resignations occur during the first few years of employment.

---

### 👨 Gender Analysis

Male employees contribute to a larger share of total attrition.

---

### 📊 Workforce Analysis

Interactive filters allow HR managers to explore attrition across departments, genders, and job roles.
